# Stage 01 — Discovery & Validation

## Purpose
Validate that the problem you're solving is real, worth solving, and technically feasible.

## Preconditions
- You have an initial idea, problem statement, or market signal
- There is stakeholder intent or strategic direction

## What to Produce
1. Discovery Brief (discovery-brief.md)
2. Research folder
3. Feasibility Assessment (feasibility-assessment.md)

## Human Gate
Before moving to design, a human must check:
- [ ] The problem is real and worth solving
- [ ] Research supports it
- [ ] No blockers in feasibility

## What the Agent Can Help With
- Research competitors
- Draft the brief from your input
- Generate feasibility checks

## Tips
Don't overthink it. Talk to people. Be honest about feasibility.
